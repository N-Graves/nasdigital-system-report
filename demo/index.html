System report

What the computer you are on will admit to. Useful before a call-out — copy this into your message and the specs arrive before anyone gets in a van.

Reading what the browser will tell us…

A website cannot see what drivers you have installed. No browser exposes that, to any page, ever — so anything claiming to scan for outdated drivers is either guessing from your graphics card model or trying to sell you something. Get them from the people who made the hardware: NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel.

Why some of it says "not available"

Browsers differ in what they will admit to, and some deliberately hide things to make you harder to track. Firefox with resistance to fingerprinting turned on reports its graphics card as "Mozilla", which this tool treats as hidden rather than repeating back at you. Memory is rounded and capped at 8 GB by every browser that reports it at all, so a 64 GB machine says "8 GB or more" — which is why it is worded that way rather than stated flatly.

The browser-age check is an estimate, and says so

Rather than a list of current version numbers baked in — which would be wrong within a month of being written, and confidently wrong within three — it works forward from a known release date at each browser's own release cadence. If your version is ahead of that estimate it says nothing, because that means this tool is behind, not you. And past a certain age it stops guessing altogether and tells you so.

Nothing here is uploaded

Every figure is read in your own browser and stays there. The Copy button puts the report on your clipboard so you can paste it wherever you like — the page never sends it anywhere, and makes no network requests at all.
